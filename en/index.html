How it works

Up and running in minutes

01

The trainer signs in

Open the trainer app, sign in with Google or email and add your clients.

02

The client gets a code

Turn on the client zone for a client and send them a link with their access code – e.g. via WhatsApp.

03

Everything is connected

Sessions, plans, measurements and messages sync between you live. You plan, your client sees it.

Installation

No App Store – straight to your home screen

Lift Tara is a web app. Open it in your browser and add it to your home screen – it then works like a regular full-screen app.

iPhone

In Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

Android

In Chrome, open the ⋮ menu → Install app.

Computer

In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon in the address bar.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does the client need to create an account?

No. The client gets an access code from the trainer and uses it to sign in to the client zone. No sign-up, no password.

Do I need to download the app from the App Store or Google Play?

No. Lift Tara is a web app – open it in your browser and add it to your home screen with one tap. From then on it works like a regular app.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. The trainer app works offline and your changes sync as soon as you are back online.

Can I use the app on my phone and my computer?

Yes. Once you sign in, your data syncs across all your devices – iPhone, Android and computer.

What does the client see and what does the trainer see?

The client sees their sessions, plan, measurements, records and messages. The trainer sees all their clients, the calendar, finances and everything clients log.

Can a client's access be revoked?

Yes. The trainer can revoke the access code or issue a new one at any time – the old code then stops working.
