Installation

No App Store, straight to your home screen

Lift Tara is a web app. Open it in your browser and add it to your home screen – then it behaves like a regular app, full screen too.

iPhone

In Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

Android

In Chrome open the menu ⋮ → Install app.

Computer

In Chrome or Edge click the install icon in the address bar.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Lift Tara suitable for beginners?

Yes. Choose “Beginner” in the questionnaire and you get a simpler program with fewer sets. Every exercise has step-by-step instructions and common mistakes.

Do I have to go to the gym?

No. The program can also be built for home with dumbbells or with no equipment at all.

What is the difference between the plans?

Basic has a ready-made full-body program, the exercise library and live workouts. Medium adds a program tailored to your goal, custom workouts and full progress tracking. Pro is the full version with an AI assistant, which is coming soon.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes, anytime in the app under Settings → Subscription or via the link in your email. If you cancel during the 7-day trial, you pay nothing. Your data stays saved.

Do I need to download it from the App Store or Google Play?

No. Lift Tara is a web app – open it in your browser and add it to your home screen with one tap. Then it behaves like a regular app.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes. Workouts and logging work offline and your changes sync when you’re back online.

Can I use it on my phone and computer?

Yes. Once you sign in, your program, workouts and progress sync across all your devices.
