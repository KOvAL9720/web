Terms of service

1. What Lift Tara is

Lift Tara is a web app for personal trainers. The trainer app is used to manage clients, sessions, plans, finances and messages. The client zone lets the trainer's clients see their plan, sessions and progress. The client zone is free for clients.

2. Subscription and price

  • The trainer app is used on a subscription basis for €15 per month. Any applicable VAT is calculated at checkout based on the customer's country.
  • The first 7 days are free. A card or other payment method is entered at checkout; the first payment is taken only after the trial ends, unless you cancel before then.
  • The subscription renews automatically every month until you cancel it.
  • We will announce any price change by email at least 30 days in advance. The new price applies from the next billing period.

3. Payments

Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the merchant of record. Lemon Squeezy issues the receipt and handles taxes. The Lemon Squeezy buyer terms also apply to your purchase. We never see or store your card details.

4. License key

After you order, you receive a license key by email, which unlocks the app. The key is for one trainer and their account; do not share it with other trainers. When the subscription ends, the key stops working.

5. Your data

The data you enter in the app belongs to you. You can download it as a backup at any time (Settings → Download backup). After the subscription ends, your data stays saved and you get it back when you renew. We will delete it on request.

Privacy policy

Who processes the data

Controller: Jakub Kovalčík, Slovakia, kubo.kovalcik@gmail.com.

What data and why

  • Trainer account – email and name from signing in with Google or email. Used to sign in and to sync data between devices.
  • Data in the app – clients, sessions, plans, measurements, payments and messages entered by the trainer. Stored on the device and in the cloud so it syncs and so the client can see it in their zone. Gallery photos stay on the device only.
  • Subscription – the buyer's email and license key, used to verify the subscription.
  • Client zone – the client signs in with a code from the trainer; they only see what the trainer shares with them and can log their own workouts, habits and messages.

We process data to provide the app (performance of a contract) and as required by law. We do not sell it, we do not use it for advertising, and neither the website nor the app uses tracking or advertising cookies.

Data of the trainer's clients

The trainer is responsible for their clients' data (they are its controller). Lift Tara processes it only on the trainer's instructions so the app can work.

Who else processes data

  • Google Firebase – sign-in and cloud data storage.
  • Lemon Squeezy – payments and license keys.
  • GitHub Pages – hosting of the website and the app.
  • Only if the trainer turns them on: Google Calendar (copying sessions to the calendar) and ntfy.sh (phone notifications).

Some of these providers may process data outside the EU, based on standard contractual clauses or other safeguards under the GDPR.

How long

We keep data for as long as you have an account. When you ask us to delete it, we delete it within 30 days. Payment records are kept by Lemon Squeezy according to its legal obligations.
